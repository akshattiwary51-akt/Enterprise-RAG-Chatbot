# SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA — Compensation Policy (HR only)

## Salary Bands
The senior engineer salary band is 140000 to 185000 per year. The junior developer salary band is 70000 to 95000 per year.
Compensation reviews take place every March and are confidential to HR and the review committee.

## Bonus Policy
The annual bonus target is 10 percent of base salary for all grades. Bonuses are paid in April.
