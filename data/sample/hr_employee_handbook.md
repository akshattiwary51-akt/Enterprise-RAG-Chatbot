# SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA — Employee Handbook (all staff)

## Working Hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Remote work is allowed up to three days per week.

## Vacation
Full-time employees receive 25 vacation days per year. Unused days up to 5 may be carried over.

## Security Basics
Report lost devices to IT within 24 hours. Never share passwords or one-time codes.
